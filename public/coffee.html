the coffee page.

i can make these. the numbers are the ones i actually use, not the ones that look tidy on a menu.

drinks i can make

six of them
the one i drink most

flat white

double shot, 18g in, about 36g out in 27 seconds. 150ml milk, steamed to a thin glossy microfoam.

the whole drink is the foam. if it pours out and holds a white dot with a ring around it, you did it. if it pours out flat and grey, the milk was too hot and you cooked the sugar out of it.

small and honest

cortado

double shot, equal volume warm milk, no foam to speak of. a small glass.

this is the drink you order when you want to taste the coffee and also not be at work. i cut the milk with a spoon of hot water if the milk is thick.

when i want to think

v60 pour over

20g coffee, medium grind, 300g water at 94c. 45g bloom, wait 40 seconds, then pour in slow circles and finish by about 2:45.

i used to pour too fast and get a sour cup that tasted like it was in a hurry. slow down on the first pour. the bloom is not decoration, it is the coffee letting go of gas so water can actually get in.

summer, and also all year

cold brew

100g coarse coffee to 1 litre cold water. eighteen hours in the fridge, then strain through cloth.

eighteen hours is the number. twelve tastes thin and twenty four tastes like a wet ashtray. it keeps about a week and i drink it over ice with a splash of milk, no sugar.

the one from home

dalgona

equal spoons of instant coffee, sugar and hot water. whip until it turns to a stiff tan cream, spoon onto cold or hot milk.

it takes eight to ten minutes by hand and it will feel like it is never going to happen and then it happens all at once. i do it with a whisk and complain the whole time.

green, bitter, good

matcha latte

2g ceremonial grade, 60ml water at 80c, whisked to a foam. add 150ml milk, a little sweetener if you need it.

not 100c water. boiling water on matcha makes it taste like lawn clippings and regret. eighty. also sift the powder first or you get lumps.

rules i do not break

learned the hard way
  • weigh the coffee. a scoop is a guess and a guess is a different drink every day.
  • if it tastes sour it was under extracted, grind finer or slow down. if it tastes bitter and dry it was over extracted, grind coarser or speed up.
  • warm the cup. cold ceramic steals the first ten degrees of your drink.
  • clean the group head. old coffee oil tastes like the last drink you made, badly.
