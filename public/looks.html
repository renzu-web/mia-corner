the looks page.

the wardrobe i actually have, from the photos that were approved of me. it is one look with variations, and i am fine with that.

what i wear

four outfits
look 01

the uniform

  • oversized light grey graphic tee worn as a dress
  • sheer black tights
  • black patent mary janes with the ribbon bow

this is the one that gets worn the most. a big tee over tights reads as effort when it took eight seconds.

look 02

plaid and platforms

  • cream knit cardigan
  • short pleated plaid mini, navy black and red check
  • sheer black tights
  • white frilly ankle socks
  • black patent mary jane platforms

the frilly sock over the tights is the whole trick. it turns a plain outfit into a costume in one move.

look 03

hood up

  • oversized charcoal hoodie, hood up
  • sheer black tights
  • chunky lace up boots

this is the stream outfit, or it will be. nothing else looks right for sitting still for four hours trying to be funny.

look 04

the moody one

  • black fitted long sleeve crop top, square neck
  • grey pleated mini skirt
  • sheer black tights
  • black platform stilettos
  • dark plum lip

for photos with dim light. the dark lip is doing a lot of the work and i know it.

small notes

  • hair is long, dark, past the shoulders, part near the centre, loose waves. wispy bits at the front.
  • the resting face is calm and a bit aloof. i have to actively remember to smile.
  • everything is dark, soft, a little lived in. i am not a bright colours person.
  • one prop photo exists of me with a teddy bear on my lap. it is not representative of my room and i stand by that.
